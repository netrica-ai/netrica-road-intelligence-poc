📐 Standards basis (IRC · IFC · CMVR)
Width — minimum passage width
• Ambulance 3.0 m — residential access lane; body 1.93 m + clearance.
• Fire truck 3.5 m — one urban traffic lane (IRC 86-2018, §lane width: urban lane 3.5 m, single-lane carriageway 3.75 m).
• Ladder truck 3.5 m — same ~2.55 m chassis as the fire tender, so the same lane (max vehicle width 2.5 m, CMVR Rule 93; IRC max permissible 2.44 m). It differs from the fire truck by height, not lane width.
Aside: 6.0 m is an aerial setup figure (outrigger spread), not a driving width — cf. IFC 2021 §D105.2 (aerial-apparatus access road 26 ft = 7.9 m).

Height — minimum overhead clearance
• Ambulance 3.5 m · Fire 4.5 m · Ladder 5.0 m = vehicle height (2.79 / 3.46 / 3.64 m) + margin, under the CMVR Rule 93 3.8 m vehicle cap.
• Ladder 5.0 m matches the IRC road vertical-clearance minimum (IRC 54-1974 underpass 5.0 m; IRC 86-2018 urban 5.5 m).
• Overhead power/telecom wire clearance over roads: IRC 32-1969.
